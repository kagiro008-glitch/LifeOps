03 / DAY-ONE KNOWLEDGE↗

What LifeOps knows at the start

  • Nothing about the student by default. No school, timetable, name, grades, or assignments are preloaded.
  • The student supplies the current request in this chat; tasks and preferences are only learned from the student's own messages. The visible input and receipt time are logged.
  • Account memory: when signed in, tasks and chat messages are stored in the student's Supabase account and protected by row-level security. Without an account, tasks stay in this browser only.
  • Clock: server time in IST for relative-date questions; no external time event or calendar feed. A school timetable is not connected.
  • Future onboarding: a student would enter preferences directly; an official timetable would require a school-authorized source and guardian/student consent first.

Who pays?

Proposed buyer: the school, on an annual per-active-student subscription. Pilot price hypothesis: ₹99 per student per month. Today, teachers and parents coordinate homework reminders across school channels and manual lists; the cost is staff/parent follow-up time, which the pilot must measure before claiming savings.

SCHOOL-PAYS · NOT A MINOR'S PAYMENT
03
1k

First 1,000 students.

Recruit five schools through principal/teacher champions, with 200 opt-in students each. Run a two-week class pilot; show the companion in parent orientation and school community groups. First try: turn one real assignment into a plan. Week-three habit: a student-owned weekly reset and visible progress.

5 SCHOOLS × 200 OPT-INS · A DISTRIBUTION TEST
04
!

What could kill it?

Wrong deadlines erode trust. Privacy or consent failures involving minors end the pilot. Students may not return after novelty fades; schools may not pay or share distribution; voice can fail in noisy classrooms or across languages. Pilot against each risk and stop if accuracy, safety or week-three retention misses the bar.
